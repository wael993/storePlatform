# Rivo — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea**: Two interlocking L-shapes are the banks of a river. The cranked white gap is the current — information moving through the business.
- **Versions**: primary horizontal · stacked · symbol-only · wordmark-only
- **Files**: masters in this folder. Product copies live in `web/store-platform-frontend/public/`. Use `rivo-symbol-small.svg` at 16–24 px. Use `rivo-symbol-reversed.svg` on dark tiles.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 × channel width** around the logo on all sides (the white gap through the mark). The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 28 mm wide |
| Symbol | 24 px (use small-size / favicon files below 24 px) | 8 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current | #176560 | 23 101 96 | 77 0 5 60 | 7715 C | Logo, key UI, app-icon tile |
| Ink | #12262A | 18 38 42 | 57 9 0 84 | Black 7 C | Type, one-colour logo |
| Paper | #F4F1EA | 244 241 234 | 0 1 4 4 | Warm Gray 1 C | Backgrounds |

**On dark UI**, use white or **Current Light #3D9A93** — not Current on Ink (contrast is too low).

**Approved pairs**: Current on Paper · Ink on Paper · white on Current · white on Ink · black on white.
On photos, put the white or Ink version on a calm area, or sit the mark in a rounded tile.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark is constructed geometry (not a font). Do not type “rivo” to replace it.
- Product UI: existing system stack. Headlines may use a geometric sans; do not recreate the wordmark from it.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch or squash · don't recolour outside the palette · don't rotate the channel · don't fill the gap · don't add shadows, outlines or gradients · don't rearrange lockup parts · don't place on a busy background without a tile · don't recreate the wordmark by typing it · don't use calculators, coins, charts or a standalone letter R as a substitute mark.

## 7. Contact
Master files: `brand/rivo-logo/kit/` (`rivo-symbol.svg` is the drawing). Product: `public/rivo-logo.svg`, favicon, and app icons.
Trademark search is still required before public launch.
