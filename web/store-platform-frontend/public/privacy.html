2. What data we process

  • Cloud backup connection: if a store owner connects Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive (see section 4).

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • To provide the platform under the contract with the store (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Cloud backups (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)

A store owner can choose to back up the store's data to their own cloud storage. This is optional and only starts after the owner connects an account and agrees on the provider's consent screen.

  • Access we request: for Google Drive only the drive.file permission, which lets the app see and manage only the files the app itself creates. We cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive. For Dropbox and OneDrive we only use an app-specific folder.
  • What we store: an access token (encrypted with AES-256-GCM) and the email address of the connected account, so that we can show which account is connected.
  • What we upload: encrypted backup files of the store's own data. The files can only be read by the platform to restore the store's data.
  • How we use it: only to create, list, download (for restore) and delete the backup files of that store. Nothing else.
  • Sharing: we do not share data received from Google, Dropbox or Microsoft with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Revoking access: the owner can disconnect the account in Settings → Backup at any time, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be removed in the account settings of the provider, e.g. myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your storage stay there until you delete them.

Rivo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cloud backups are kept in the owner's storage according to the retention setting of the store and are deleted by the platform when they become older than that setting.
