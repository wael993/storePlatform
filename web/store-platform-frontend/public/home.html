Rivo

Rivo is a store management platform for retail and wholesale businesses. It helps store owners and their teams manage stock, sales, purchasing, customers, suppliers and store operations in one place.

Sign in to Rivo

What Rivo does

  • Products and stock: manage products, categories and stock across warehouses.
  • Sales and purchasing: create selling and buying invoices, transfers and exchanges.
  • Customers and suppliers: keep track of customers, partners and open balances.
  • Employees and roles: give each team member only the access they need.
  • Reports: see sales, stock and profit for your business.
  • Works offline: keep selling when the internet is down; changes sync when you are back online.

Cloud backups with Google Drive

Store owners can back up their store's data to their own Google Drive (or Dropbox or OneDrive) and restore it later. Rivo only asks for permission to manage the files it creates itself (drive.file) and cannot see any other files in your Drive. Backups are encrypted before they are uploaded. You can disconnect your account at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
